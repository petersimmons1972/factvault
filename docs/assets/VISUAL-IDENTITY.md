# Visual Identity: factvault

## Theme
Precision reference instrument: the visual language of 1960s NASA mission documentation — technical authority without sentimentality, the look of a Saturn V flight manual or a Mercury program mission patch. The aesthetic is competence, not decoration. Every element earns its place the way an instrument-panel annotation does.

## Typography mood
- **Display:** condensed slab serif (Roboto Slab, Courier, IBM Plex Mono Bold) reminiscent of IBM Selectric teletype output and offset-printed mission documentation.
- **Body:** clean sans-serif with generous tracking (Inter, Helvetica Neue).
- **Data values & code:** monospace, always. Telemetry-readout aesthetic.
- No light weights. This is the era of offset printing and mimeograph, not digital refinement.

## Visual personality
Right angles, ruled grids, tight tolerances. Color used as a signal: amber means "note this," off-white means "nominal." Diagrams look drafted on a light table, not generated by software. No gratuitous gradients, no rounded corners, no soft shadows. White space is structural, not decorative.

## Imagery style
NASA technical document illustrations, Apollo/Mercury/Gemini mission patches, engineering schematics, telemetry-readout facsimiles, hand-drafted orbital diagrams. Photography sourced from the NASA archives (public-domain US government works): launch facilities, Mission Control consoles, hardware close-ups, blueprint reproductions. Black-and-white with occasional amber/gold highlight tinting. No stock photography. No contemporary or AI-generated photographic imagery. Public-domain Library of Congress technical drawings are acceptable.

## SVG constraints
- `viewBox`: 750 420 unless content demands otherwise
- Corner treatment: sharp (0px) — no rounded corners anywhere
- Stroke weights: 1px / 2px / 4px only — pick one per diagram and hold
- Gradient style: flat, or very subtle linear top-to-bottom suggesting document aging
- Font family in SVGs: `'Roboto Slab', 'Courier New', Georgia, serif` for display; `'Inter', 'Helvetica Neue', sans-serif` for body
- Grid: 24px base grid where alignment matters
